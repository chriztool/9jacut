9jaCut

A free video editor made in Naija, for creators and small businesses.

Version

What 9jaCut does

9jaCut turns long recordings, phone videos and stream footage into short, ready-to-post clips for TikTok, Reels, YouTube Shorts and WhatsApp status. It also makes business promo videos from your photos in a few clicks.

Everything is free: no watermark, no subscription, no account, and no feature you start using suddenly gets locked behind a paywall. Your videos never leave your computer.

Features

✂️ Cut & reframe

Mark in and out to cut clips. Reframe any clip to vertical 9:16, square, 4:5 or your own shape, and drag to choose what stays in shot.

💬 Auto-captions

Turns speech into on-screen captions automatically. Works in English and Pidgin, Yoruba, Hausa and more, with an option to translate into English. Fix any word before you export.

🔤 Text

Add titles and messages with your own colour, size, position and style (outline, box or plain), and choose when each one appears.

🎨 Looks

Eight colour looks including Naija Gold, Cinematic and Black & white, plus brightness, contrast and saturation.

⚡ Speed & motion

Slow-mo down to 0.25x or fast-forward up to 4x, plus smooth zoom-in and zoom-out effects.

🎵 Sound

Add your own music (it can loop to fill the clip), record a voiceover with your microphone, adjust volumes and reduce background noise.

😀 Stickers

Drop emoji stickers anywhere on the video and set when they show.

🎬 One finished video

Join all your clips into one video with transitions such as crossfade, slide, wipe or circle. Clips of a different shape sit on a blurred background.

🏪 Promo videos

Fill in your business name, hours, address and contact, add photos, pick one of 52 styles and export a vertical promo video.

💾 Projects & export

Save and reopen projects, undo any change, and export from 720p up to 4K.

How to make a video

  1. Click Open Video (or drag a video onto the window).
  2. Play to the start of a good moment and press Mark In (or I), then play to the end and press Mark Out (O).
  3. Click + Add Clip (Enter). Repeat for every moment you want.
  4. On each clip, pick a shape (for example Vertical 9:16) and click Reframe to choose what stays in shot.
  5. Open ✨ Effects on a clip for captions, text, speed, looks, zoom, fades, music and voiceover. The player shows your changes as you play.
  6. Under Export settings, choose the resolution, and tick Join all clips into one video if you want a single video.
  7. Click Choose Export Folder, then Export. The folder opens when it's done.

Your privacy

9jaCut works on your own computer. Your videos, recordings and projects are never uploaded anywhere. The only time it uses the internet is the first time you use auto-captions: it downloads the free speech-recognition model once (about 116–208 MB), and after that captions work offline too.
