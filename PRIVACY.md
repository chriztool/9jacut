# 9jaCut Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 6 October 2026_

9jaCut is a free video editor for iPhone, Windows and other computers, made by Christian Agbugba.

## The short version

9jaCut does not collect, store, sell or share any information about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## Your videos, photos and recordings

- Everything you edit stays on your device. 9jaCut never uploads your videos, photos, music, voiceovers or projects anywhere.
- On iPhone, 9jaCut only reads the photos and videos you choose in the picker, and copies them into the app's own storage so your projects keep working.
- When you export, 9jaCut saves the finished video to your Photos library (if you allow it) and can open the share sheet so you can send it where you want.
- The microphone is only used while you record a voiceover, and the recording is saved on your device.

## Internet use

The iPhone app does not need the internet. The desktop app only goes online the first time you use auto-captions, to download the free speech-recognition model once; your audio is never sent anywhere.

## Children

9jaCut collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
