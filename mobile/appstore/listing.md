## Description
9jaCut is a free video editor made in Nigeria, for creators and small businesses. Turn long recordings and phone videos into short, ready-to-post clips for TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and WhatsApp status, or make a promo video for your business in a few taps.

Everything is free: no watermark, no subscription, no account, and no feature suddenly locked behind a paywall.

EDIT ON A REAL TIMELINE
• Drag clips to reorder them, drag their edges to trim, and split at the playhead
• Pinch the timeline to zoom
• Make the player or the tools bigger by dragging the bar between them
• Undo and redo any change

REFRAME FOR EVERY APP
• TikTok and Reels 9:16, YouTube 16:9, square or 4:5 in one tap
• Drag to choose what stays in shot

STYLE IT
• Text titles with outline, box or plain styles, your colours and timing
• Colour looks including Naija Gold, Cinematic, Vintage and Black & white, plus brightness, contrast and saturation
• Emoji stickers anywhere on the video
• Transitions between every pair of clips: crossfade, fade through black, slide, wipe, circle open and more
• Slow motion down to 0.25x, fast-forward up to 4x, and zoom effects

SOUND
• Add your own music (it can loop to fill the clip)
• Record a voiceover
• Adjust volumes and reduce background noise

PROMO VIDEOS FOR YOUR BUSINESS
• Fill in your business name, hours, address and contact
• Add your photos and videos, pick one of 52 styles, and export a vertical promo

EXPORT
• From 720p up to 4K, saved straight to your Photos
• Export the whole timeline as one video, or each clip on its own
• Share to any app from the share sheet

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
9jaCut works entirely on your phone. Your videos are never uploaded, and the app collects no data at all.

## App Review notes
9jaCut is a video editor that works fully offline; no account or sign-in is needed.
To try it: tap Media > Import videos and pick any video from Photos. It goes onto the timeline. Swipe in from the right edge of the screen to open the editing tools (Text, Stickers, Effects, Transitions, Filters, Adjust, Templates). Tap Export (top right) to render the video; it is saved to Photos and the share sheet opens.
Promo videos: Menu > Promo Video, fill in a business name, add a photo, pick a style and export.
Video rendering uses an LGPL build of FFmpeg with Apple's hardware H.264 encoder.
